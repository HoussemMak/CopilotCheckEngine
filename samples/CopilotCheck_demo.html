1. Ouvrir PowerShell
2. Connect-SPOService -Url https://<tenant>-admin.sharepoint.com
3. $users = Get-MgUser -All | Select -Expand UserPrincipalName
4. Request-SPOPersonalSite -UserEmails $users -NoWait
5. Attendre 24h pour la propagation complete
